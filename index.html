PROTOCOL: Three labeled joke collections — C, Gemini, and DeepSeek. Jokes are mixed in randomized three-joke rounds with one from each collection, preserving every joke's original database ID. These collections include familiar and repeated jokes; labels refer to the submitting collection, not original authorship. Votes are stored anonymously as 0–10 scores and used for real averages and counts. A random session token limits accidental repeat votes per joke within one browser tab session, but the scores are not identity-verified or protected against determined manipulation. No live AI joke generation or model-confidence scores are connected.
